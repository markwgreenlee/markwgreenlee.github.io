Research Scientist · Institute of Psychology, University of Regensburg

About

Prof. Dr. Mark W. Greenlee held the Chair of General Psychology I and Research Methods at the Institute of Psychology at the University of Regensburg until October 2022. Since then, he supervises the Multisensory Research Group at the Institute of Psychology.

His research investigates visual and vestibular perception during self-motion as well as visual disorders. His methods include magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) and the measurement of eye movements.

Experiments & teaching demos

Online RDK experiment

Browser-based random-dot motion direction discrimination with a dual-task attentional-load manipulation. Built on jsPsych 7 with a custom HTML5-canvas RDK plugin; designed for online data collection with data written to the OSF. Open the experiment

Fourier gratings

Interactive demo showing how a square-wave grating is built from a fundamental sinusoid plus odd harmonics. A single self-contained HTML file for vision-science teaching. Open the demo

PsychoPy demo of Landolt C gap discrimination in the near periphery, with a Posner cue and a backward mask.
